RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE – FICTIONAL, FOR TESTING)

This Lease is made between Oakview Property Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned resident ("Tenant") for the premises at 482 Elmwood Ave, Unit 3B ("Premises").

1. TERM. The lease begins September 1, 2026 and ends August 31, 2027. This lease automatically renews for an additional 12-month term unless Tenant gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end date.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is $2,150, due on the 1st of each month. Rent must be paid through the Landlord's online portal. A convenience fee of $4.95 applies to each online payment.

3. LATE FEES. If rent is not received by 11:59 PM on the 1st, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $100, plus $25 for each additional day rent remains unpaid.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,200. A cleaning fee of $350 will be deducted from the deposit at move-out regardless of the condition of the Premises. Landlord will return the remaining deposit within 21 days of move-out.

5. FEES AT SIGNING. Tenant shall pay a non-refundable administrative fee of $250 and a non-refundable lease preparation fee of $150 at signing.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electricity and internet. Water, sewer, and trash will be billed to Tenant as a flat monthly utility charge of $85.

7. ENTRY. Landlord and its agents may enter the Premises at any time, with or without notice, for inspections, repairs, or showings.

8. REPAIRS. Tenant is responsible for all repairs under $300, including appliance repairs. Landlord will make other repairs within a reasonable time.

9. PETS. No pets are permitted without Landlord's written approval. Approved pets require a non-refundable pet fee of $500 and monthly pet rent of $50 per pet.

10. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant ends the lease early, Tenant shall pay an early termination fee equal to three (3) months' rent, and shall remain responsible for rent until a replacement tenant is found.

11. SUBLETTING. Tenant may not sublet or assign the Premises.

12. GUESTS. Guests may not stay more than 3 consecutive nights without Landlord's written consent.

13. LIABILITY. Tenant waives any claims against Landlord for damage to Tenant's property or personal injury, including claims arising from Landlord's negligence.

14. RENTER'S INSURANCE. Tenant must maintain renter's insurance with at least $100,000 liability coverage.

15. DISPUTES. Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration selected by Landlord. Tenant waives the right to a jury trial and to participate in any class action.

16. QUIET HOURS. Quiet hours are 10 PM to 8 AM daily.

17. PARKING. One assigned parking space is included. Additional spaces are $75 per month.

18. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Lease is the entire agreement between the parties.
